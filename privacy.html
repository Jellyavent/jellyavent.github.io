Privacy Policy – JellyAvent Publisher

Last updated: 27 October 2026

1. Who we are

JellyAvent Publisher is an internal tool of JellyAvent used to publish JellyAvent's own videos to its own TikTok account. Contact: jellyavent@outlook.com

2. Data we access

When the JellyAvent account authorizes the app with Login Kit, we receive from TikTok:

  • basic profile information of the authorized account (open id, display name, avatar) – scope user.info.basic;
  • permission to upload and publish videos to that account – scopes video.upload and video.publish.

    • 3. How we use it

    We use this data only to identify the authorized JellyAvent account and to upload and publish the videos the JellyAvent team chooses. We do not access data of any other TikTok user.

    4. Storage and security

    Access tokens are stored only on the JellyAvent team's own computer, are never shared, and can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions).

    5. Sharing

    We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

    6. Deletion

    To delete the data, revoke the app's access in TikTok or email jellyavent@outlook.com; locally stored tokens are deleted within 7 days.

    7. Changes

    We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
